Understanding Air Quality Index (AQI)

AQI Categories & Health Impacts

Good (0–50)
Air quality is satisfactory and poses little or no health risk.
Satisfactory (51–100)
Minor breathing discomfort may occur for sensitive individuals.
Moderate (101–200)
Breathing discomfort may occur for people with lung or heart disease, children, and older adults.
Poor (201–300)
Increased likelihood of respiratory symptoms and reduced tolerance for outdoor activity.
Very Poor (301–400)
High risk of respiratory illness for the general population.
Severe (401–500)
Serious health effects. Emergency conditions for everyone.
